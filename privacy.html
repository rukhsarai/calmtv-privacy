CalmTV Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

CalmTV is an ambient scenes and sleep sounds app for Fire TV. We built it to collect as little as possible: in fact, we do not collect any personal information at all.

What we do not collect

CalmTV has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email address, location, viewing history or device identifiers, and we do not sell or share any data.

What stays on your device

The app stores a few settings locally on your Fire TV so it works the way you left it: the scene you played last, whether you have dismissed the first-run tip, and whether your subscription is active (so a subscriber is not locked out if the network is briefly unavailable). This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Streaming the scenes

Scene video and audio are streamed from our content delivery network (Amazon CloudFront). As with any web request, your device's IP address is necessarily visible to the network while a file is delivered. We have not enabled access logging, and we do not use this information to identify you.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Amazon through the Amazon Appstore. We never see or store your payment details. Amazon tells the app only whether a subscription is active, so it can unlock the premium scenes. Amazon's handling of your purchase is covered by the Amazon Privacy Notice.

Children

CalmTV is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date. For questions, use the developer contact details on CalmTV's Amazon Appstore listing.
